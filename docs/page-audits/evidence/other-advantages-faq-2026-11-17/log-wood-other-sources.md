## Die-Face Cutting Knives

Local support: D2, M2 HSS, T42, PM-HSS, 440C and carbide; straight/forked/stepped bodies and square/bevelled/curved edges; matched knife sets; hubs and die plates available. No universal claim that knives from underwater/water-ring/hot-face systems interchange.

1. MAAG, Die plate: https://maag.com/accessories/die-plate/ — manufacturer indexed description checked 2026-11-17. Application-specific wear-face material supports evaluating the knife and die face as a pair.
2. MAAG technical PDF, Die plates: https://maag.cn/wp-content/uploads/PTC-2022_part2_DiePlates-eng.pdf — indexed manufacturer PDF excerpt checked 2026-11-17; full open timed out. Material examples pair blade grades with particular die-face constructions. Used only to corroborate material-pair reasoning, not adopt exact material ratios or a universal hardness rule.
3. MAAG technical troubleshooting PDF: https://maag.cn/wp-content/uploads/PTC-2022_part4_trouble-1_eng.pdf — indexed PDF text checked 2026-11-17. Indexed page 24 of 58 lists excessive blade/die wear, blade tracking and cutting alignment, with possible causes including incorrect blade type, excessive contact pressure, poor alignment and abrasive feed. Supports reviewing holder alignment/contact/die condition before a grade change. Draft's uneven wear question remains a general assessment, not a claimed one-cause diagnosis. All blades sweep the shared die face; the earlier wording implying a separate permanent die area per blade was removed.
4. MAAG M-USG description cited under Strand establishes underwater strand cutting as a distinct arrangement. The word “underwater” alone therefore does not identify this product's die-face station.
5. No blanket permission to mix a single new knife with a worn set. Commercial answer quotes matched sets, hubs and die plates instead. Knife set seating discussion is drawing geometry, not an unverified machining-tolerance claim.
