## Live primary sources and their actual uses

### Guillotine Shear Blades

- https://tyzack.com/guillotine-shear-blades/ — “Guillotine shear blades”, page lines 20–28. Supports the market distinction between single-piece and segmented sets, OEM drawings and machined/ground knife bodies. All Sureay material choices and actual supply configurations come from its own catalog. Tyzack's five-metre range, tolerance, matching process guarantee and sharpening service are not used.
- https://www.tkmgroup.com/en/troubleshooting/chipping-shear-blades — “Chipping Shear Blades”, lines 17–24. Direct support for FAQ “Why do sheet-metal shear blades chip?” concerning overload, cutting clearance and double-layer feed. Also supports conditional regrinding, subject to original geometry and machine limits. It is not evidence that a particular Sureay customer's chipping was caused by those conditions.
- https://www.iksinc.com/images/pdfs/Grinding_SB_and_SC_031708.pdf — “Grinding procedures for Scrap Choppers & Shear Blades”, numbered procedure. Supports keeping geometry/parallelism relevant to clearance after grinding. No IKS numbers or Sureay grinding-service offer transferred.
- Existing catalog FAQ defines clearance versus rake and lists 1/2/4 usable-edge configurations. These remain configuration-dependent.
