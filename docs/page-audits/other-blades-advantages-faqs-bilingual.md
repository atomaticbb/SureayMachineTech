### How do hook count and cutter thickness affect replacement selection? / 刀钩数量和刀片厚度如何影响替换选型？

### When should wear resistance or toughness guide the steel choice? / 何时应优先考虑耐磨性或韧性？

### What can cause cutters to wear or chip prematurely? / 刀片为什么会过早磨损或崩口？
